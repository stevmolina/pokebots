### Changed

- In the new design of the Pokebots build, the profile button on home opens Settings: a close button,
  the account, your servers with a check on the open one (a tap opens it) and the server tools, the
  app settings, help, Sign out, and the app name and version.
- In the new design, search shows its filters as chips at the top and names the kind of each result,
  and the Marketplace shows one list with chips for apps, agents, skills and what is installed.
