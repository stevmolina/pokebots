### Added

- The chat list has an `Active` | `Folders` switch on top, as on the desktop. `Active` shows the
  `Working`, `Snoozed` and `Done` groups over the folders. `Folders` shows every agent in its folder.
  `Needs you` stays on top in both.
- Snooze an agent from its long-press menu (for 1 hour, until tomorrow, or for 1 week). It moves to
  `Snoozed`, shows how long is left, and comes back by itself. `Unsnooze` brings it back at once. The
  choice stays on this phone, on this computer's server and on a joined server.

### Changed

- The `Active` | `Folders` switch is a rounded pill with a raised thumb, and `Working` agents show as
  rounded cards with a spinner and how long they have run.
