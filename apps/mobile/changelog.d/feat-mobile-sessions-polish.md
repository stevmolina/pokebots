### Changed

- Session lists show working sessions first, then the newest. A working session has a spinner icon,
  and an idle session has an icon for its topic, such as a bug or a design, as on the desktop.
- The agent profile in the new design has three tabs, as on the desktop: Activity (Model, Computer,
  the three newest sessions and Routines), Library (Files and Skills) and Settings (Edit profile and
  notifications).

### Fixed

- The chat list no longer shows a "Cannot update a component" warning when another screen loads an
  agent's sessions.
