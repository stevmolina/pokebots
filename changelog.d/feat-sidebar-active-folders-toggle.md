### Added

- The chat list has an `Active` | `Folders` switch on top. `Active` groups the bots by state:
  `Working`, `Done` and `Snoozed`, then everything else under `Settled`. `Folders` shows every bot in
  its folder, also the ones that are working, done or snoozed. `Needs you` stays on top in both.
- Snooze a bot from its right-click menu (for 1 hour, until tomorrow, or for 1 week). It moves to
  `Snoozed`, shows how long is left, and comes back by itself when the time is up.
