### Changed

- Settings open as a centered modal over the window, not as a full page. The list on the left has eight sections: General, Appearance, Notifications, Voice, Account, This computer, Updates and Machines. Account and This computer show their pages (Profile, Billing, Mobile Connect; App behavior, Dynamic Island, Agent dock, Computer Use) as chips. Machines lists your machines, and a machine opens its own settings in the same modal, with a Back row to the list. On a phone the modal fills the screen. Links that open one settings page still open it.
