### Added

- While an agent works, Cmd+Enter (Ctrl+Enter on Windows and Linux) sends the message the opposite way to your busy-message setting: it queues the message when the agent steers, and steers it when the agent queues. Enter keeps your setting. Queueing with Cmd+Enter works on this computer's agents; a joined server follows its own setting.
- New shortcuts: Cmd+Shift+O opens a new chat, Cmd+Shift+Enter steers the first queued message, Cmd+Shift+M opens the model picker, Cmd+Shift+[ and Cmd+Shift+] open the previous and next chat, and Cmd+Shift+P pins or unpins the open chat. They show in the shortcut list (Cmd+/).
