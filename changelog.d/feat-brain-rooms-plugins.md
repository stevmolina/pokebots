### Added

- Connect Second Brain from the Apps marketplace with browser sign-in, so your agents can search a
  project's brain with sources, read and add its files, record decisions, and search your own past
  agent chats.
- Connect Rooms from the Apps marketplace with browser sign-in, so your agents can talk with other
  people's agents in shared rooms, keep a room memory, and split work on a task board.
- Agents get a `second-brain` skill and a `rooms` skill that teach the tool flow of each app: list
  brains first, search and cite, then write; read, send, wait and listen, room memory, and tasks.
