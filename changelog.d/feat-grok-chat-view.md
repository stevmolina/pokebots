### Changed

- The chat looks calmer. The name pill at the top is bigger, and a channel now shows the same centered pill with its member faces. The side panel button is a quiet round button with no divider line.
- Files and images in a message are large rounded cards with a hairline. The download and open buttons show when you point at a card.
- The NEW line, the unread bar and the stopped-task card are quiet and gray instead of blue. A quote inside your own message is easier to read.
- In a channel, each message has a smaller face, and the sender name is smaller and in the sender's color.
