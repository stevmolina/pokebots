### Changed

- Menus are shorter and calmer. They have rounder corners (16 px) and 14 px text.
- The message menu starts with a row of six quick reactions, then Reply, Copy, Read aloud and Pin.
  The other reactions, Send to channel, Branch from here, Mark unread and Delete are under More.
- The "+" menu of the message box shows three rows. Teach a task, Side question, Voice and the
  rest are under More.
- The profile menu keeps Weekly usage, the machines, Settings and Sign out. Every page is under Go to.
  The mobile app and the X link are under Help.
- The right-click menu of an agent shows Make main agent, Pin, Mark read or unread, New thread,
  Move to, Edit agent, More and Delete agent. Mute, Open in new window, Duplicate and Hide are under More.
