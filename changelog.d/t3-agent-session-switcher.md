### Changed

- Each agent shows one chat at a time. The tab row at the top of the chat is gone. The header shows
  the agent as a pill with its face, its name and the open session, and "+" starts a new session.
- The session that you open or start last is the main session. The agent shows it when you open the
  agent. The other sessions keep running in the background.
- Click the pill to open the agent panel. Its new Sessions card lists the main session first, then
  the newest others, with their state and unread dot. Rename, stop, open beside the chat, archive and
  delete are in the "..." menu of each row. "All sessions" shows the rest and the archived sessions.
- The agent panel is calmer: the face, the name and an "Edit" button; then Sessions; then one card
  with Library (files, memories and tables), Computer and Routines; then Notifications. "Edit" opens
  every other setting, as before. This works on a joined server and in the web client too.
